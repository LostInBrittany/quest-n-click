# Baseline: v4.1.20 "Last Mile"

What 4.1.20 measures, and against what. **This sheet holds no measure of the final candidate**: it is committed before
that candidate runs, so it cannot name its run. The candidate writes its own baseline from its jobs
(`tools/release/baseline.mjs`, in `candidate-gate`, reading every job through `tools/release/run-jobs.mjs`), puts it
in its manifest, attests it, and the release publishes it as `web-scumm-v4.1.20-baseline.json` and `.md`. A measure
whose job left nothing is written there "not measured on this candidate", never guessed. The 4.1.19 column is
`4.1.19.md`'s (candidate 38172871690), not this release's. The thresholds below were written before the technical
candidate ran (`changes/4120-release.log.md`), so no candidate is judged against a criterion younger than it.

## What the candidate's baseline holds

As 4.1.19's (`4.1.19.md`), and from 4.1.20: the three loads per store with their median, minimum and maximum, and
the comparison with `load.json`; the full qualification's steps by id; the backup memory per size and store; the nine
mutation sets with their exemptions by category; the signing rehearsal; every job of the run, read page by page.

## Not measured in 4.1.20, said as such

- A run of 24–48 h behind a real domain (#77): its thresholds are `4.1.18.md`'s; it is a people's pass of 4.2.0-rc.
- Runner speed: the three loads of a store run on one runner, so one slow runner can read as a reproduced
  regression (#120: ×2.11 against ×0.78 on #121, the same code). A speed probe or loads spread across runners: 4.2.
- A restore inside 512 MiB beyond about 20 MiB of backup (about 10 MB of memory per MiB backed up, 20 MB for a
  restore): measured and documented in REALITY-OPS; reducing it is 4.2's.
