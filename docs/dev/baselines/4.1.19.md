# Baseline: v4.1.19 "Field Closure"

What 4.1.19 measures, and against what. **This sheet holds no measure of the final candidate**: it is committed before
that candidate runs (plan §12), so it cannot name its run. The candidate writes its own baseline from its jobs
(`tools/release/baseline.mjs`, in `candidate-gate`), puts it in its manifest, attests it, and the release publishes
it as `web-scumm-v4.1.19-baseline.json` and `web-scumm-v4.1.19-baseline.md`. A measure whose job left nothing is
written there "not measured on this candidate", never guessed. The 4.1.18 column is `4.1.18.md`'s (candidate
38047300736, PR #73), not this release's.

## What the candidate's baseline holds (plan §12.2)

The SHA and `GITHUB_RUN_ID`; the runner's OS and image, Node; the date; the cache (warm: npm and the mutation reports
of identical inputs); the test declarations and the proofs from the commit's ratchet; each heavy attempt; both loads
and both leaderboards; every mutation set's score and input hash; the qualification's steps; the external installs'
and the connector scenarios' jobs; each archive's and SBOM's size and SHA-256; the image's identities; the people's
passes (not-run); each job's duration, the wall time and the longest job.

## Not measured in 4.1.19, said as such

- A run of 24–48 h behind a real domain (#77): its thresholds are `4.1.18.md`'s.
- Reproducibility bit for bit across toolchains: the archives are compared by their canonical content manifest
  (files, modes, sizes, inner SHA-256); bit for bit only on the candidate's fixed toolchain (D33).
- The cold-cache duration of the candidate.
