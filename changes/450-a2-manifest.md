### Changes

- **The documentation's map, `wiki/manifest.json`, and `npm run wiki`** (4.5.0). Every page of the coming bilingual wiki has an entry: 213 pages across the domains of the plan's catalogue, all `planned` for now. Each entry gives a stable id, a type, an audience, a context, its French and English titles, its public URLs (`EN-Create-Rooms`, `FR-Creer-Salles`), the source files it rests on and the questions a reader would ask. `npm run wiki -- check` keeps the manifest, the pages and their links consistent; it fails on errors only and lists the links to pages not written yet. `stats` counts the pages. `build --target wiki` writes the folder the GitHub wiki will be published from: a bilingual home page, a sidebar per language, a footer, each ready page under its URL, and indexes by task, symptom, identifier and vocabulary. The repository also gains a bilingual glossary (`wiki/data/glossary.json`, with synonyms such as lease, bail and verrou) and page templates (`wiki/_templates/`). Nothing is published yet.
